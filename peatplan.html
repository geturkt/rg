Disclaimer. This tool implements Ray Peat's dietary framework (carbohydrate-dominant, PUFA-minimised, dairy/gelatin-centred protein). That framework is heterodox: several tenets are not supported by mainstream nutrition evidence and some conflict with conventional glucose management. Nutrient values are approximate averages for typical preparations and vary by brand, cut and ripeness. Not medical advice — review any high-sugar eating pattern with your clinician, especially if you monitor blood glucose.
